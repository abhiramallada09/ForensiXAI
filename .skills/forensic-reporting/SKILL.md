# Forensic Reporting Skill

Standardizes court-admissible and investigation-grade documentation:
- Generate structured reports containing:
  - Case metadata & investigator credentials
  - Executive summary and scope of investigation
  - Evidence repository inventory with SHA-256 integrity hashes
  - Detailed artifact and memory analysis breakdown
  - Chronological reconstructed timeline
  - Anti-forensic indicators & AI inferences with confidence intervals
  - Explicit research limitations and investigator notes
- Maintain strict formatting, clear pagination, evidence provenance tables, and PDF/JSON/CSV export compatibility.
